# B3 Prototype session kit

For the 5 prototype sessions in the research plan (section B3), between build weekends 2 and 3, once the bar works on the default sites. Each session takes about 30 minutes.

## Setup, per session

- A fresh browser profile with the test build loaded unpacked, so earlier sessions leave no data behind
- Run at least one of the five sessions in Brave. In one sec's reviews, freezes and bypasses are what earn 1 star
- Ask before recording the screen. If they say no, take notes only
- The observation sheet below, one per person
- The participant enters their own phone: model, purchase date, price. An estimate is fine; note when they had to guess

## Script

Opening:

> Ik test een browserextensie, niet jou. Als iets onduidelijk is, ligt dat aan het ontwerp. Wil je hardop denken: zeggen wat je ziet, wat je verwacht en wat je denkt? Tijdens de opdrachten help ik je niet, want ik wil juist zien waar het misgaat.

### Task 1: install and set up (5 min)

> Installeer deze extensie en stel hem in zoals je dat thuis zou doen.

Say nothing about the permission prompt. Watch what they do when it appears.

### Task 2: browse (5 to 8 min)

> Stel, je bent nieuwsgierig naar de nieuwe Pixel of iPhone. Kijk rond op Tweakers Pricewatch en Coolblue zoals je dat normaal zou doen.

Don't mention the bar. If they notice it, let them carry on; ask about it afterwards.

### Task 3: the card (only if they didn't open it themselves)

> Klik eens op je telefoon in de balk bovenaan. Wat zie je?

### Questions about the buttons

Point at each one:

> Wat denk je dat deze knop doet?

Ask about "Close tab", "Log urge" and "Cooldown", or whatever the build calls them.

### Closing questions

1. Waardoor zou je dit uitzetten?
2. Zou je het laten staan? Waarom wel of niet?
3. Werd de pagina op een moment traag, of haperde er iets?
4. Point at the cost per month, on the bar or the card: "Wat zegt dit bedrag je? Wat zou je ermee doen?" Note whether they read it as a reason to keep the phone or to sell or trade it in now.

Log "would keep it" as said, with no weight (research plan rules).

## Tally for Checkpoint A

| Observation | Count | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hesitated at or declined the permission prompt | __ of 5 | 2 or more: rewrite the explanation shown before it |
| Said the bar gets in the way | __ of 5 | 2 or more: one collapsed line by default |
| Misread "Close tab" | __ of 5 | 2 or more: rename it |
| Misread "Log urge" | __ of 5 | 2 or more: rename it |
| Misread "Cooldown" | __ of 5 | 2 or more: rename it |
| Any slowdown or freeze | __ of 5 | Any: fix before the field window |
| Read the cost line as a reason to sell now | __ of 5 | 2 or more: reword it before B4, for example drop "and falling" |

The freeze row comes from the adoption scan: reliability, not the idea, is what gets the closest comparable tool uninstalled. The cost-line row comes from the Reddit reading, where a trade-in offer was read as "the most that phone will ever be worth".
